---
name: Infinity AI replacement readiness
description: Product-level guidance for making Infinity AI feel like a dependable ChatGPT replacement rather than a collection of demos.
---

Infinity AI should be evaluated by complete workflows—ask, attach context, get a strong answer, use tools when needed, recover from failure, and return to the work later—not by the number of standalone modes or panels.

**Why:** Current research against ChatGPT's official capabilities showed that Infinity AI has many individual capabilities, but mode fragmentation, provider variability, weak project/file continuity, and unusually long research flows make it feel less finished than a simpler but more cohesive assistant.

**How to apply:** Before adding a new studio, mode, or agent surface, strengthen the canonical chat flow, model reliability, user-owned persistence, multimodal files, citations, and recoverable tool execution.
